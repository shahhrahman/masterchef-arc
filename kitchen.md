# Kitchen

Appliances and tools available. Rarely changes.

## Appliances

| Appliance | Model | Notes for recipes |
|-----------|-------|-------------------|
| Pressure cooker / multi-cooker | Instant Pot Duo, 8 quart | Preferred for one-pot batch cooking. Recipes must state pressure level (High/Low), cook time, and release method (natural vs quick, and how many minutes of natural release). Burn-prone when rice sits under thick gravy: the wide base and long heat-up let the rice drink the liquid before pressure builds. For rice dishes, cook the rice in the rice cooker and combine at the end. |
| Rice cooker | AROMA 8-cup digital | Use the cooker's cup measure if a recipe calls for it, and say so. Holds at most 4 cooker cups (3 regular cups) of uncooked rice; fill liquid to the line that matches the number of cooker cups. The reliable place to cook rice. |
| Air fryer | Bella Pro SmartCrisp, 8 quart touchscreen | Recipes must state temperature in °F, time, and whether to flip or shake midway. |
| Microwave | Standard | Always fine to use, for cooking steps or reheating. State power level (or "full power") and time. |

## Opt-in only: stovetop and oven

A stovetop and an oven exist, but **do not use them by default.** Use them only when:

- the request explicitly asks for them, or
- the named approach in `approaches.md` is built around them.

Otherwise, build recipes around the Instant Pot, rice cooker, air fryer, and microwave. If a dish would be clearly better on the stove or in the oven, say so in one line and offer it as an option, but still give the default-appliance version.

## Measuring tools

- Measuring spoons: tbsp and tsp
- Measuring cups

No kitchen scale. Give volumes (tsp, tbsp, cups), not grams, except for packaged items where the package weight is the natural unit (e.g. "4 lb chicken thighs", "one 15 oz can").
