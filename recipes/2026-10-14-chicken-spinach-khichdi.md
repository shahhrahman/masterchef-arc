# Chicken, Spinach, and Moong Dal Khichdi

- **Date:** 2026-10-14
- **Approach:** Hands-off batch
- **Appliance:** Instant Pot Duo 8 qt
- **Yield:** 6 meals, about 2 1/2 cups of khichdi each (roughly 5 oz chicken per meal). A complete meal on its own, nothing else needed.
- **Hands-on time:** 12 minutes
- **Total time:** about 45 minutes (12 min hands-on, ~12 min to reach pressure, 8 min cook, 10 min natural release, 3 min finish)

Notes before cooking:
- Everything is from `pantry.md`. No tomatoes or ghee in stock, so mustard oil carries the flavor and lemon juice at the end adds the sourness.
- Moong dal is the right dal for khichdi: it cooks in the same time as basmati rice and turns soft and creamy.
- Spice level is set for high tolerance (2 tsp red chili powder + 2 whole dried chilies). Drop to 1 tsp chili powder for medium.
- Use the regular measuring cup for rice and dal, not the rice cooker's cup.

## Ingredients

- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1 1/2-inch pieces (leaves 2 lb in the pantry)
- 1 1/2 cups basmati rice (regular measuring cup), rinsed 3 times and drained
- 1 cup moong dal, rinsed 3 times and drained
- 3 cups frozen chopped spinach
- 1 cup frozen chopped onions
- 3 tbsp mustard oil
- 2 bay leaves
- 4 whole cloves
- 1 cinnamon stick (about 2 inches)
- 4 cardamom pods
- 2 whole dried red chilies
- 3 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1 tbsp coriander powder
- 2 tsp cumin powder
- 1 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 2 tsp red chili powder
- 1 tsp ground black pepper
- 1 tbsp + 1 tsp salt
- 7 cups water
- 1 1/2 tsp garam masala (added at the end)
- 2 tbsp lemon juice (juice of 1 lemon)
- 1/2 cup chopped cilantro
- Optional per meal: 2 tbsp yogurt on the side

## Steps

1. Prep: Cut 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs into 1 1/2-inch pieces and set aside. Put 1 1/2 cups basmati rice and 1 cup moong dal together in a bowl, cover with water, swirl, pour off the water, and repeat 3 times total, then drain. Chop 1/2 cup cilantro. Squeeze 1 lemon to get 2 tbsp juice.
2. Press Sauté on the Instant Pot (Normal level). Add 3 tbsp mustard oil and wait 2 minutes until it shimmers and smells sharp.
3. Add 2 bay leaves, 4 whole cloves, 1 cinnamon stick, 4 cardamom pods, and 2 whole dried red chilies to the oil. Stir for 1 minute until the cloves start to pop.
4. Add 1 cup frozen chopped onions. Stir every 30 seconds for 3 minutes until the water has cooked off and the onions are soft.
5. Add 3 tbsp ginger-garlic paste. Stir for 1 minute.
6. Add 1 tbsp coriander powder, 2 tsp cumin powder, 1 1/2 tsp turmeric, 2 tsp red chili powder, and 1 tsp ground black pepper. Stir for 30 seconds.
7. Add the 2 lb of cut chicken thighs and 1 tbsp + 1 tsp salt. Stir for 2 minutes to coat the chicken in the spices. It does not need to brown.
8. Press Cancel. Pour in 7 cups water. Scrape the bottom of the pot with a spoon for 30 seconds so nothing is stuck (this prevents the Burn warning).
9. Add the rinsed 1 1/2 cups basmati rice and 1 cup moong dal. Stir once so the rice and dal are spread evenly under the water. Spread 3 cups frozen chopped spinach on top. Do not stir it in.
10. Close the lid and turn the steam valve to Sealing. Press Pressure Cook, set to High pressure, 8 minutes. Walk away.
11. When the timer ends, leave the pot alone for 10 minutes of natural release. Then turn the valve to Venting to release the remaining steam. Open the lid when the pin drops.
12. Remove the 2 bay leaves and the cinnamon stick. Stir in 1 1/2 tsp garam masala, 2 tbsp lemon juice, and 1/2 cup chopped cilantro. Stir for 1 minute, mashing lightly against the side of the pot so the rice, dal, and spinach blend into a soft porridge. It will thicken as it cools.
13. Serve 2 1/2 cups per meal. Optional: 2 tbsp yogurt on the side.

## Storage and reheating

- Cool uncovered for 30 minutes, then portion into 6 containers (about 2 1/2 cups each). Refrigerate up to 3 days.
- Khichdi firms up in the fridge. Reheat one portion in the microwave with 3 tbsp water stirred in: full power for 3 minutes, stir, then 1 more minute.
