# Chicken, Spinach, and Moong Dal Khichdi

- **Date:** 2026-10-14
- **Approach:** Hands-off batch
- **Appliance:** Instant Pot Duo 8 qt
- **Yield:** 6 meals, about 2 1/2 cups of khichdi each (roughly 5 oz chicken per meal). A complete meal on its own, nothing else needed.
- **Hands-on time:** 12 minutes
- **Total time:** about 45 minutes (12 min hands-on, ~12 min to reach pressure, 8 min cook, 10 min natural release, 3 min finish)

Notes before cooking:
- Everything is from `pantry.md`. No tomatoes or ghee in stock, so mustard oil carries the flavor and lemon juice at the end adds the sourness.
- Moong dal is the right dal for khichdi: it cooks in the same time as basmati rice and turns soft and creamy.
- Spice level is set for high tolerance (2 tsp red chili powder + 2 whole dried chilies). Drop to 1 tsp chili powder for medium.
- Use the regular measuring cup for rice and dal, not the rice cooker's cup.
- Prep is organised into 4 bowls (approaches.md rules 7 and 8). Have all four ready before pressing Sauté.

## Ingredients

Grouped by the prep bowl they go into (see Prep below).

Bowl 1, whole spices:
- 2 bay leaves
- 4 whole cloves
- 1 cinnamon stick (about 2 inches)
- 4 cardamom pods
- 2 whole dried red chilies

Bowl 2, chicken with spice mix:
- 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs, cut into 1 1/2-inch pieces (leaves 2 lb in the pantry)
- 1 tbsp coriander powder
- 2 tsp cumin powder
- 1 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 2 tsp red chili powder
- 1 tsp ground black pepper
- 1 tbsp + 1 tsp salt

Bowl 3, rice and dal:
- 1 1/2 cups basmati rice (regular measuring cup)
- 1 cup moong dal

Bowl 4, finish:
- 1 1/2 tsp garam masala
- 2 tbsp lemon juice (juice of 1 lemon)
- 1/2 cup chopped cilantro

Straight from the bag or jar, no bowl needed:
- 3 tbsp mustard oil
- 1 cup frozen chopped onions
- 3 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 7 cups water
- 3 cups frozen chopped spinach
- Optional per meal: 2 tbsp yogurt on the side

## Prep (before any heat is on)

Four bowls total. Each bowl goes into the pot in one go, so nothing has to be dug out from under something else.

- **Bowl 1 (whole spices, small bowl):** 2 bay leaves, 4 whole cloves, 1 cinnamon stick, 4 cardamom pods, 2 whole dried red chilies.
- **Bowl 2 (chicken with spice mix, large bowl):** Cut 2 lb boneless skinless chicken thighs into 1 1/2-inch pieces and put them in the bowl. Measure directly on top of the chicken: 1 tbsp coriander powder, 2 tsp cumin powder, 1 1/2 tsp turmeric, 2 tsp red chili powder, 1 tsp ground black pepper, 1 tbsp + 1 tsp salt. Stir with a spoon for 30 seconds until every piece is coated. This is the spice mix; it goes in with the chicken, so it lives in the same bowl.
- **Bowl 3 (rice and dal, medium bowl):** 1 1/2 cups basmati rice and 1 cup moong dal together. Cover with water, swirl, pour off the water, and repeat 3 times total. Drain and leave in the bowl.
- **Bowl 4 (finish, small bowl):** 1 1/2 tsp garam masala, 2 tbsp lemon juice (squeeze 1 lemon), 1/2 cup chopped cilantro. Stir once. It can sit at room temperature until the end.

Not bowled: 3 tbsp mustard oil, 1 cup frozen chopped onions, 3 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, 7 cups water, and 3 cups frozen chopped spinach are measured straight from the bottle, bag, or jar at their step. The onions and spinach go in at different times, so keep them in the freezer until needed.

## Steps

1. Press Sauté on the Instant Pot (Normal level). Add 3 tbsp mustard oil and wait 2 minutes until it shimmers and smells sharp.
2. Tip in all of Bowl 1 (2 bay leaves, 4 whole cloves, 1 cinnamon stick, 4 cardamom pods, 2 whole dried red chilies). Stir for 1 minute until the cloves start to pop.
3. Add 1 cup frozen chopped onions straight from the bag. Stir every 30 seconds for 3 minutes until the water has cooked off and the onions are soft.
4. Add 3 tbsp ginger-garlic paste straight from the jar. Stir for 1 minute.
5. Tip in all of Bowl 2 (2 lb cut chicken thighs coated in the spice mix: 1 tbsp coriander powder, 2 tsp cumin powder, 1 1/2 tsp turmeric, 2 tsp red chili powder, 1 tsp ground black pepper, 1 tbsp + 1 tsp salt). Scrape the bowl so no spice is left behind. Stir for 2 minutes. The chicken does not need to brown.
6. Press Cancel. Pour in 7 cups water. Scrape the bottom of the pot with a spoon for 30 seconds so nothing is stuck (this prevents the Burn warning).
7. Tip in all of Bowl 3 (the rinsed 1 1/2 cups basmati rice and 1 cup moong dal). Stir once so the rice and dal are spread evenly under the water. Spread 3 cups frozen chopped spinach on top, straight from the bag. Do not stir it in.
8. Close the lid and turn the steam valve to Sealing. Press Pressure Cook, set to High pressure, 8 minutes. Walk away.
9. When the timer ends, leave the pot alone for 10 minutes of natural release. Then turn the valve to Venting to release the remaining steam. Open the lid when the pin drops.
10. Remove the 2 bay leaves and the cinnamon stick. Tip in all of Bowl 4 (1 1/2 tsp garam masala, 2 tbsp lemon juice, 1/2 cup chopped cilantro). Stir for 1 minute, mashing lightly against the side of the pot so the rice, dal, and spinach blend into a soft porridge. It will thicken as it cools.
11. Serve 2 1/2 cups per meal. Optional: 2 tbsp yogurt on the side.

## Storage and reheating

- Cool uncovered for 30 minutes, then portion into 6 containers (about 2 1/2 cups each). Refrigerate up to 3 days.
- Khichdi firms up in the fridge. Reheat one portion in the microwave with 3 tbsp water stirred in: full power for 3 minutes, stir, then 1 more minute.
