# Tofu, Chickpea, and Spinach Curry with Basmati Rice

- **Date:** 2026-10-22
- **Approach:** Hands-off batch, sized for 4 meals
- **Appliances:** Instant Pot Duo 8 qt (curry), AROMA rice cooker (rice), both running at the same time
- **Yield:** 4 dinners, each about 1 2/3 cups rice and 1 3/4 cups curry
- **Hands-on time:** about 30 minutes (15 minutes of chopping and bowls, 10 minutes at the pot, 5 minutes to finish and portion)
- **Total time:** about 1 hour

Notes before cooking:
- Over the Hands-off batch 15-minute guide on purpose: you asked to chop onions, and two fresh red onions are worth it here.
- Mustard oil, mustard seeds, and spinach make this lean Bengali rather than North Indian. Olive oil works if you would rather not have the mustard bite.
- Tofu goes in straight from the block, patted dry and cubed. It firms up and soaks up the curry under pressure. If you want crisp edges and have 12 spare minutes, air fry the cubes at 400°F for 12 minutes, shaking at 6, and stir them in at the end instead of pressure cooking them.
- Chickpeas are drained and rinsed so their canning liquid does not foam under pressure.
- Spice level is high: 2 serranos, 2 dried red chilies, 1 tsp chili powder. For medium, drop to 1 serrano and 1/2 tsp chili powder.
- Do not freeze this one. Tofu turns spongy after freezing. Four meals over two days is inside the 3-day fridge window anyway.

## Ingredients

Grouped by the prep bowl they go into (see Prep below).

Rice cooker:
- 3 rice-cooker cups basmati (the cup that came with the cooker; this equals 2 1/4 regular cups), rinsed in the cooker pot
- water to the cooker's line 3 (no salt or butter in the cooker; the rice is salted through the curry and buttered at the end)

Bowl 1, whole spices:
- 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
- 2 whole dried red chilies
- 1 bay leaf

Bowl 2, onions:
- 2 medium red onions, chopped (about 2 cups)
- 2 serrano peppers, chopped small

Bowl 3, paste and spice mix:
- 1 1/2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1 1/2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp red chili powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
- 2 1/2 tsp salt

Bowl 4, everything that cooks under pressure:
- 1 block firm tofu, 14 oz, patted dry and cut into 3/4-inch cubes (a 12 or 16 oz block changes nothing)
- one 15 oz can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- one 10 oz package grape tomatoes, halved
- 2 cups frozen chopped spinach (about 10 oz), straight from the freezer
- 1/4 cup cashews

Straight into the pot:
- 3 tbsp mustard oil
- 3/4 cup water

To finish, off heat:
- 1/2 cup yogurt
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter, for the rice

## Prep (before any heat is on)

- **Rice cooker first, before any chopping:** measure 3 rice-cooker cups of basmati into the cooker's inner pot. Cover with cold water, swirl for 10 seconds, pour off, repeat until rinsed 3 times, then pour off as much water as you can. Add water up to line 3 and nothing else. Close and press White Rice. About 30 minutes; it switches to Warm by itself.
- **Bowl 1 (whole spices), small bowl:** 1/2 tsp mustard seeds, 2 whole dried red chilies, 1 bay leaf.
- **Bowl 2 (onions), medium bowl:** peel and chop 2 medium red onions into roughly 1/2-inch pieces, about 2 cups. Chop 2 serranos small and add them on top.
- **Bowl 3 (paste and spice mix), small bowl:** 1 1/2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste. Measure onto it: 1 1/2 tsp coriander powder, 1 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp red chili powder, 1 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp ground black pepper, 2 1/2 tsp salt.
- **Bowl 4 (pressure stage), large bowl:** pat the tofu block dry with a paper towel and cut it into 3/4-inch cubes. Open the chickpea can, drain it, rinse the chickpeas under the tap, and add them. Halve the grape tomatoes and add them. Add 2 cups frozen spinach and 1/4 cup cashews.
- **By the pot:** 3 tbsp mustard oil in the measuring cup, 3/4 cup water in a glass.

## Steps

Every step self-contained: restate quantity, setting, temperature, and time inside the step.

1. Instant Pot on Sauté, Normal (its default setting). Pour in 3 tbsp mustard oil. Heat for 2 minutes. It may smoke a little; that is expected.
2. Add Bowl 1 [1/2 tsp mustard seeds, 2 dried red chilies, 1 bay leaf]. Stir for 30 seconds, until the seeds crackle.
3. Add Bowl 2 [2 chopped red onions, about 2 cups, and 2 chopped serranos]. Sauté on Normal for 6 minutes, stirring every 2 minutes and scraping the bottom each time. The onions soften and turn golden at the edges.
4. Add Bowl 3 [1 1/2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste with 1 1/2 tsp coriander powder, 1 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp red chili powder, 1 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp black pepper, 2 1/2 tsp salt]. Stir for 1 minute.
5. Add 3/4 cup water and scrape the bottom for 30 seconds so nothing stays stuck. Add Bowl 4 [14 oz cubed tofu, one 15 oz can of chickpeas drained, 10 oz halved grape tomatoes, 2 cups frozen spinach, 1/4 cup cashews]. Stir once, gently, so the tofu does not break. Press Cancel.
6. Lid on, valve to Sealing. Pressure Cook, High, 4 minutes. When it beeps, do nothing for 10 minutes (natural release), then turn the valve to Venting and wait until the pin drops (about 1 minute). Open.
7. Press Cancel and wait 2 minutes so the curry stops bubbling. Add 1/2 cup yogurt and 1/4 cup chopped cilantro. Fold gently for 30 seconds. Done.
8. Rice: when the cooker has been on Warm for at least 10 minutes, open it, add 1 tbsp unsalted butter, and fluff with the spatula for 30 seconds so the butter melts through. Portion into 4 containers, about 1 2/3 cups rice each (1 level cup plus a heaped serving spoon), with about 1 3/4 cups curry alongside or on top.

## Storage and reheating

- Cool uncovered for 30 minutes, then lid the containers and refrigerate. Eat within 3 days. Do not freeze (the tofu goes spongy).
- Reheat: sprinkle 1 tbsp water over the rice, lid resting loosely on top, microwave full power 3 minutes, stir, then 1 more minute.
- If a meal tastes flat, stir in 1/4 tsp salt and a squeeze of lemon after reheating.
