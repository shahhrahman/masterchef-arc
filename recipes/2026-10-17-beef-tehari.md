# Bangladeshi-Style Beef Tehari with Potatoes

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Approach:** Weekend project
- **Appliance:** Instant Pot Duo 8 qt, two pressure stages
- **Yield:** 8 meals, about 2 1/2 cups each (roughly 4 1/2 oz cooked beef and 3 oz potato per meal), plus about 2 cups of extra gravy for spooning over
- **Hands-on time:** about 1 hour total: 10 minutes the night before for the marinade, about 50 minutes on cook day
- **Total time:** marinade 1 hour minimum (overnight is better), then about 2 hours on cook day (20 min Sauté, ~15 min to pressure, 20 min cook, 12 min release, 10 min gravy handling, ~12 min to pressure, 5 min cook, 12 min release, 5 min rest, 10 min portioning)

Notes before cooking:
- Tehari is classically made with kalijira (chinigura) rice. Basmati from the pantry stands in; the method keeps the grains firm and separate.
- No tomatoes: tehari gets its tang from yogurt.
- Potatoes: petite yellow potatoes have thin skins that are meant to be eaten. Rinse them, keep the skin on, halve them, and they cook on top of the rice in the second pressure stage. Potatoes in tehari are a Dhaka tradition, so this is not a stretch.
- Rice is 3 1/2 cups (not 4) because the potatoes add bulk and the pot sits near its 2/3 line at the rice stage. Portions stay about the same size.
- Mustard oil is the signature flavor. It smells sharp while it heats; that is normal.
- Spice level is extra spicy: 2 tsp red chili powder plus 36 serranos, 18 of them slit. For plain hot, slit 8. For medium, slit none.
- Prep is 5 bowls (approaches.md rules 7 and 8). Two of them get washed and reused mid-recipe; the steps say when.
- "Sauté on More" below means: press Sauté, then press Sauté again (or Adjust on older Duos) until the "More" light is on.
- The Weekend project extra mile here is the marinade, 16 minutes of attended Sauté, and a gravy-measuring step between the two pressure stages that makes the rice come out right every time.

## Ingredients

Grouped by the prep bowl they go into (see Prep below).

Bowl 1, marinade:
- 3 lb beef stew meat
- 1 cup yogurt
- 3 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 2 tsp red chili powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 1/2 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp ground black pepper
- 1 tbsp salt

Bowl 2, whole spices:
- 3 bay leaves
- 6 cardamom pods
- 2 cinnamon sticks (about 2 inches each)
- 6 whole cloves

Bowl 3, onions:
- 4 medium yellow onions (about 1 1/2 lb), sliced 1/4 inch thick (about 6 cups)

Bowl 4, rice:
- 3 1/2 cups basmati rice (regular measuring cup, not the rice cooker cup), rinsed and drained

Bowl 5, rice-stage additions:
- 1.5 lb petite yellow potatoes (the whole bag), rinsed, skin on, halved
- 36 serrano peppers (three 4 oz bags), stems on, 18 slit lengthwise, 18 left whole
- 2 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 tsp garam masala

Straight into the pot:
- 1/2 cup mustard oil
- 1 cup water (beef stage)
- 1 3/4 cups water (rice stage)

To serve:
- 2 lemons, cut into quarters (1 quarter per meal)
- 1/2 cup chopped cilantro (1 tbsp per meal)

## Prep (before any heat is on)

- **Bowl 1 (marinade), large bowl, the night before or at least 1 hour ahead:** put in all 3 lb of beef. Add 1 cup yogurt, 3 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, 2 tsp red chili powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 2 tsp coriander powder, 1 1/2 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp ground black pepper, 1 tbsp salt. Stir with a spoon for 1 minute until every piece is coated. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour; overnight is better.
- **Bowl 2 (whole spices), small bowl:** 3 bay leaves, 6 cardamom pods, 2 cinnamon sticks, 6 cloves.
- **Bowl 3 (onions), large bowl:** peel 4 medium onions, cut each in half from top to root, lay flat, slice 1/4 inch thick. About 6 cups.
- **Bowl 4 (rice), large bowl:** 3 1/2 cups basmati. Cover with cold water, swirl with your hand for 10 seconds, tilt and pour the cloudy water off while holding the rice back with your hand. Repeat until you have rinsed 3 times. Pour off as much water as you can. Leave the rice in the bowl.
- **Bowl 5 (rice-stage additions), large bowl:** rinse the whole 1.5 lb bag of potatoes under the tap, keep the skin on, and cut each one in half. Add 36 serranos, stems on: slit 18 of them lengthwise from the tip to just below the stem, leave 18 whole. Add 2 1/2 tsp salt and 1 tsp garam masala on top and toss with your hands for 20 seconds so the potato cut faces pick up the seasoning.
- **By the pot:** 1/2 cup mustard oil in the measuring cup, 1 cup water in a glass. The 1 3/4 cups water for the rice stage is measured later, in step 9.

## Steps

Every step self-contained: restate quantity, setting, temperature, and time inside the step.

1. Instant Pot on Sauté, set to More. Pour in 1/2 cup mustard oil. Heat for 3 minutes. It will shimmer and may smoke a little; that is expected.
2. Add Bowl 2 [3 bay leaves, 6 cardamom pods, 2 cinnamon sticks, 6 cloves]. Stir for 30 seconds.
3. Add Bowl 3 [4 sliced onions, about 6 cups]. Sauté on More for 10 minutes, stirring every 2 minutes and scraping the bottom each time. They shrink and turn golden at the edges.
4. Add Bowl 1 [3 lb beef with all of its marinade]. Stir for 30 seconds to mix with the onions. Sauté on More for 6 minutes, stirring every 2 minutes.
5. Add 1 cup water. Scrape the bottom for 30 seconds so nothing stays stuck (this prevents the Burn warning). Press Cancel.
6. Lid on, valve to Sealing. Pressure Cook, High, 20 minutes. When it beeps, do nothing for 10 minutes (natural release), then turn the valve to Venting and wait until the pin drops (about 2 minutes). Open. While it cooks, wash Bowl 1 and Bowl 3 with soap and keep them by the pot.
7. Beef out: with a large spoon, lift the beef pieces into the washed Bowl 1, letting the gravy drip back into the pot. Leave the onions, spices, and gravy in the pot. About 3 minutes.
8. Measure the gravy: with the 1-cup measuring cup, scoop the gravy (onion bits and spices included) into the washed Bowl 3, one level cup at a time, counting. It is hot; hold the cup by the handle. Expect 4 to 6 cups. When the cup can no longer pick anything up, stop; a thin film left in the pot is fine. Pour exactly 3 cups from Bowl 3 back into the pot. Whatever stays in Bowl 3 is the extra gravy for serving. If you got less than 3 cups in total, pour it all back and add water to reach 3 cups.
9. Add 1 3/4 cups water to the pot. Add Bowl 4 [3 1/2 cups rinsed rice]. Stir once, gently, so the rice is level and under the liquid, scraping the bottom as you go. Spread Bowl 5 [1.5 lb halved potatoes, 36 serranos, 2 1/2 tsp salt, 1 tsp garam masala] over the rice, then spread the beef from Bowl 1 on top. Do not stir again. The pot is now close to its 2/3 line; that is expected, add nothing else.
10. Lid on, valve to Sealing. Pressure Cook, High, 5 minutes. When it beeps, do nothing for 10 minutes (natural release), then turn the valve to Venting and wait until the pin drops (about 2 minutes). Open.
11. Press Cancel. Fold gently from the bottom with a spatula for 1 minute so beef, potatoes, rice, and chilies mix. Leave the lid off and rest for 5 minutes.
12. Portion into 8 containers, about 2 1/2 cups each (2 level cups plus a heaped serving spoon), making sure each gets 5 or 6 potato halves. Eat one now with a quarter lemon squeezed over, 1 tbsp cilantro, and 2 tbsp of the extra gravy from Bowl 3.

## Storage and reheating

- Cool uncovered for 30 minutes, then lid the containers. 6 meals go in the fridge (eat within 3 days). 2 meals go in the freezer the same day, with 2 tbsp of the extra gravy spooned over each before freezing; keep up to 1 month. Frozen potatoes come back slightly mealy; in tehari that is fine.
- Extra gravy: in a jar in the fridge. Spoon 2 tbsp over each fridge meal before reheating.
- Reheat from the fridge: sprinkle 1 tbsp water over the meal, lid resting loosely on top, microwave full power 3 minutes, stir, then 1 more minute.
- Reheat from frozen: thaw in the fridge overnight, then reheat as above. In a hurry: microwave 50% power 6 minutes, stir, then full power 2 minutes.
- If a meal tastes flat, stir in 1/4 tsp salt and a squeeze of lemon after reheating.
