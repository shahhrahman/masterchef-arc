# Tandoori Chicken, 9-Egg, and Vegetable Masala Khichdi

- **Date:** 2026-11-15
- **Approach:** Hands-off batch, sized for 3 meals
- **Appliance:** Instant Pot Duo 8 qt
- **Yield:** 3 large meals, about 3 cups each (3 eggs and about 1/3 cup shredded tandoori chicken per meal)
- **Hands-on time:** 18 minutes (10 minutes chopping and prepping 4 bowls, 5 minutes initial Sauté, 3 minutes egg-and-chicken fold at the end)
- **Total time:** about 45 minutes (10 min prep + 5 min Sauté, ~10 min to reach pressure, 8 min cook, 10 min natural release, 3 min finish)

Notes before cooking:
- **Uses 9 eggs at once:** Whisking 9 eggs and folding them into the bubbling-hot khichdi on Sauté at the end creates thick, soft egg ribbons throughout the pot—no peeling boiled eggs, and scrambled curds reheat safely in the microwave without yolks popping.
- **Why the tandoori chicken goes in at the end:** Since the 7 oz restaurant tandoori chicken is already cooked, folding it in with the eggs for the final 3-minute Sauté warms it through and lets its smoky spice melt into the pot without pressure-cooking the meat into dry strings.
- **Separate rice option:** If you want separate rice and dal instead of a one-pot khichdi, cook the 1 cup basmati rice (1 1/3 rice-cooker cups, water to line 1 1/3) in the AROMA rice cooker and reduce the water in Step 4 from 5 cups to 3 cups.
- **No fishing required:** Bowl 1 uses only mustard seeds and whole dried red chilies—no bay leaves, cloves, or cinnamon sticks to pick out later.
- **Spice and salt level:** High heat (2 serranos, 2 dried red chilies, 1 tsp red chili powder). Salt is set to 2 1/4 tsp total across the pot because the restaurant tandoori chicken is already salted.
- **Herb swap:** Uses fresh parsley in place of out-of-stock cilantro for the finishing herb.

## Ingredients

Exact measurements for everything, all from `pantry.md` plus the 7 oz leftover tandoori chicken. Grouped by prep bowl.

Bowl 1, whole spices:
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 2 whole dried red chilies

Bowl 2, chopped aromatics, paste, and spice mix:
- 2 medium red onions (about 12 oz), peeled and chopped into 1/2-inch pieces (about 2 cups)
- 2 Roma tomatoes, chopped into 1/2-inch pieces (about 1 1/2 cups)
- 2 serrano peppers, stems removed, chopped small
- 1 1/2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 1/2 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp red chili powder
- 1 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
- 2 tsp salt

Bowl 3, rice and dal:
- 1 cup basmati rice (regular measuring cup)
- 1 cup moong dal (regular measuring cup)

Bowl 4, eggs and tandoori chicken (goes in after pressure cooking):
- 9 large eggs
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 7 oz cooked restaurant tandoori chicken, pulled off the bone and shredded into bite-size pieces (about 1 cup meat)

Straight from the bottle, tap, or freezer (no bowl needed):
- 2 tbsp mustard oil
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter
- 5 cups water
- 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables (carrots, green beans, corn, peas), straight from the freezer
- 2 cups frozen chopped spinach, straight from the freezer
- 1 tbsp lime juice (juice of 1/2 lime)
- 1/3 cup chopped parsley
- Optional per meal: 2 tbsp yogurt on the side

## Prep (before any heat is on)

One line per bowl. Group everything that enters the pot at the same moment into the same bowl (approaches.md rules 7 and 8).

- **Bowl 1 (whole spices, small bowl):** Measure 1 tsp mustard seeds and 2 whole dried red chilies into a small bowl. Kept separate so the seeds can crackle in hot oil before the wet vegetables go in.
- **Bowl 2 (aromatics, paste, and spice mix, medium bowl):** Chop 2 medium red onions (about 2 cups), 2 Roma tomatoes (about 1 1/2 cups), and 2 serrano peppers into the same medium bowl. Add 1 1/2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, then measure the spice mix directly on top: 2 tsp coriander powder, 1 1/2 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp red chili powder, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp ground black pepper, and 2 tsp salt. No need to stir yet.
- **Bowl 3 (rice and dal, medium bowl):** Measure 1 cup basmati rice and 1 cup moong dal into one bowl. Cover with cold water, swirl for 10 seconds, pour off the water, and repeat 3 times total; drain well. Kept separate because they go in after the aromatics are sautéed and the pot is deglazed.
- **Bowl 4 (eggs and tandoori chicken, large bowl):** Crack 9 large eggs into a large bowl, add 1/4 tsp salt, and whisk with a fork for 20 seconds until blended. Pull the meat off the 7 oz cooked tandoori chicken (discarding any bones or black char), shred into bite-size pieces (about 1 cup), and drop the chicken right into the whisked eggs. Kept separate because both go in after pressure cooking so the eggs scramble softly and the cooked chicken stays tender.
- **By the Instant Pot:** Have 2 tbsp mustard oil, 1 tbsp unsalted butter, 5 cups water, 1/2 lime (for 1 tbsp juice), and 1/3 cup chopped parsley ready. Leave the frozen mixed vegetables and frozen chopped spinach in the freezer until Step 5.

## Steps

Every step self-contained: restate quantity, setting, temperature, and time inside the step.

1. Press Sauté on the 8 qt Instant Pot and leave it on Normal. Add 2 tbsp mustard oil and 1 tbsp unsalted butter, and wait 90 seconds until the butter melts and the oil shimmers.
2. Tip in all of Bowl 1 [1 tsp mustard seeds, 2 whole dried red chilies]. Stir for 30 seconds on Sauté Normal until the mustard seeds pop.
3. Tip in all of Bowl 2 [2 chopped red onions, 2 chopped Roma tomatoes, 2 chopped serrano peppers, 1 1/2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, 2 tsp coriander powder, 1 1/2 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp red chili powder, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp ground black pepper, 2 tsp salt]. Cook on Sauté Normal for 5 minutes, stirring every minute and pressing the tomatoes with your spoon so they break down into a thick masala.
4. Press Cancel. Pour in 5 cups water and scrape the bottom of the pot with your spoon for 30 seconds so nothing is stuck (this prevents the Burn warning).
5. Tip in all of Bowl 3 [rinsed 1 cup basmati rice and 1 cup moong dal] and stir once to level them under the water. Pour 1 cup frozen mixed vegetables and 2 cups frozen chopped spinach straight from the freezer bags on top in an even layer—do not stir them down.
6. Close the lid and turn the steam valve to Sealing. Press Pressure Cook, set to High pressure, and set the time to 8 minutes.
7. When the timer beeps, leave the pot alone for 10 minutes of natural release, then turn the valve to Venting to release the remaining steam (about 1–2 minutes). Open the lid and press Cancel.
8. Press Sauté on Normal. Wait 30 seconds for the khichdi to bubble, then pour in all of Bowl 4 [9 whisked eggs with 1/4 tsp salt and 1 cup shredded tandoori chicken] in a slow stream while stirring slowly from the bottom with your spoon. Cook on Sauté Normal for 3 minutes, folding every 20 seconds, until the eggs set into soft, creamy ribbons throughout the porridge and the chicken is piping hot. Press Cancel.
9. Off heat, squeeze in 1 tbsp lime juice (juice of 1/2 lime) and add 1/3 cup chopped parsley. Fold for 30 seconds. Portion into 3 containers (about 3 cups each). Serve with 2 tbsp yogurt on the side if desired.

## Storage and reheating

- Cool uncovered for 30 minutes, then lid the containers and refrigerate for up to 3 days.
- Khichdi thickens considerably in the fridge. To reheat one portion, stir in 3 tbsp water, rest the lid loosely on top, and microwave at full power for 2 minutes, stir well, then microwave at full power for 1 more minute.
