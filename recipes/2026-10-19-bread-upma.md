# Bread Upma with Tandoori Chicken

- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Approach:** single packed lunch, weekday effort (no named approach for this yet; sized for one main meal)
- **Appliance:** Instant Pot Duo 8 qt, Sauté only, no pressure
- **Yield:** 1 large lunch, about 4 cups
- **Hands-on time:** about 17 minutes (7 minutes prep, 10 minutes at the pot)
- **Total time:** about 20 minutes, plus 10 minutes cooling before packing

Notes before cooking:
- The chicken is leftover takeout tandoori chicken, about 7 oz with bones, not a pantry item. Pull the meat off the bones and discard the bones and any fully black char. Expect about 1 cup of shredded meat.
- Frozen chopped onions are used on purpose: no onion to slice. They will not brown; they soften and release water, which is exactly what dry bread needs.
- Spice level is high: 2 serranos plus chili powder, on top of a chicken that is already spiced. For medium, use 1 serrano.
- Salt is modest, 1/2 tsp, because the bread and the chicken are already salted.
- "Sauté on Normal" means: press Sauté and leave it at its default setting (the "Normal" light).
- Best made in the morning if you have 20 minutes. Made the night before, it still packs fine; see the reheating line.

## Ingredients

Grouped by the prep bowl they go into (see Prep below).

Bowl 1, tempering:
- 1/2 tsp mustard seeds
- 2 tbsp peanuts

Bowl 2, vegetables and spice:
- 1 cup frozen chopped onions
- 2 serrano peppers, chopped small
- 1 cup grape tomatoes (about 12), halved
- 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp red chili powder
- 1/2 tsp cumin powder
- 1/2 tsp coriander powder
- 1/4 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp salt

Bowl 3, bread and chicken:
- 4 slices multigrain bread, cut into 1-inch squares (about 4 cups)
- about 1 cup shredded tandoori chicken (the whole takeout portion, meat only)

Straight into the pot:
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp unsalted butter
- 1/4 cup water, plus 2 tbsp more if needed

To finish, off heat:
- juice of 1/2 lemon
- 2 tbsp chopped cilantro

## Prep (before any heat is on)

- **Bowl 1 (tempering), small bowl:** 1/2 tsp mustard seeds, 2 tbsp peanuts.
- **Bowl 2 (vegetables and spice), medium bowl:** 1 cup frozen chopped onions straight from the bag, 2 serranos chopped small, 12 grape tomatoes halved, 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste. Measure onto the top: 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp red chili powder, 1/2 tsp cumin powder, 1/2 tsp coriander powder, 1/4 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp salt. No need to stir.
- **Bowl 3 (bread and chicken), large bowl:** pull the tandoori chicken off the bones, shred it with your fingers into bite-size pieces, about 1 cup. Cut 4 slices of bread into 1-inch squares and add them on top.
- **By the pot:** 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp butter, 1/4 cup water in a glass, half a lemon, 2 tbsp chopped cilantro.

## Steps

Every step self-contained: restate quantity, setting, temperature, and time inside the step.

1. Instant Pot on Sauté, Normal. Add 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp unsalted butter. Wait 1 minute for the butter to melt and foam.
2. Add Bowl 1 [1/2 tsp mustard seeds, 2 tbsp peanuts]. Stir for 1 minute. The seeds will crackle and the peanuts darken a shade.
3. Add Bowl 2 [1 cup frozen chopped onions, 2 chopped serranos, 12 halved grape tomatoes, 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp red chili powder, 1/2 tsp cumin powder, 1/2 tsp coriander powder, 1/4 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp salt]. Sauté on Normal for 6 minutes, stirring every 2 minutes and pressing the tomatoes with the spatula so they collapse. The onions thaw, soften, and release water; that is expected.
4. Add 1/4 cup water and scrape the bottom for 20 seconds. Add Bowl 3 [4 cups bread squares, about 1 cup shredded tandoori chicken]. Fold gently from the bottom for 2 minutes on Sauté Normal, so every bread square is coated and turns soft. Press Cancel. If any squares still feel dry and firm, sprinkle 2 tbsp water over them and fold for 30 seconds more.
5. Off heat: squeeze the juice of 1/2 lemon over the pot and add 2 tbsp chopped cilantro. Fold for 20 seconds. Leave the lid off and cool for 10 minutes before packing.

## Storage and reheating

- Pack in one container once it has cooled 10 minutes. It eats fine at room temperature.
- With a microwave at lunch: sprinkle 1 tbsp water over it, lid resting loosely on top, full power 90 seconds, stir.
- Made the night before: refrigerate overnight, and use the microwave line above with 2 tbsp water instead of 1.
- The 24 oz loaf will not be finished by its 2026-10-27 date. Freeze half today; frozen slices go straight into the air fryer at 350°F for 4 minutes to toast.
