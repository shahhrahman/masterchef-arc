# Air Fryer Spiced Shrimp, Okra, and Red Onions with Basmati Rice

- **Date:** 2026-11-12
- **Approach:** Hands-off batch, sized for 3 meals
- **Appliance:** Bella Pro SmartCrisp 8 qt air fryer (shrimp, okra, onions), AROMA 8-cup digital rice cooker (rice)
- **Yield:** 3 meals, about 1 1/2 cups cooked rice and 1 1/2 cups roasted shrimp, okra, and onions each (roughly 8 oz raw / 6 oz cooked shrimp per meal)
- **Hands-on time:** 15 minutes (10 minutes chopping onions and tossing 2 bowls, 5 minutes loading, shaking, and portioning)
- **Total time:** about 35 minutes (rice cooker and air fryer run in parallel)

Notes before cooking:
- **Why the air fryer works so well here:** Roasting frozen cut okra at 400°F evaporates its moisture fast so the pods blister and char without turning slimy.
- **Two-stage timing:** Frozen cut okra and fresh red onions need 15 minutes total at 400°F to blister and sweeten, while frozen shrimp only need 7 minutes so they do not turn rubbery. Giving Bowl 1 (okra and onions) an 8-minute head start before adding Bowl 2 (shrimp) means everything finishes at the exact same moment.
- **Shrimp bag size:** Written for 1 1/2 lb frozen shrimp (about 8 oz raw per meal across 3 meals). If your bag is 1 lb (about 5.3 oz per meal), keep all oil, spice, temperature, and time settings identical.
- **Oil choice:** Mustard oil gives a sharp South Asian roast flavor; swap in equal amounts of olive oil (2 1/2 tbsp total) if you prefer a milder base.
- **Spice level:** High (2 tsp red chili powder + 1/2 tsp black pepper across the two bowls). Drop to 1/2 tsp red chili powder per bowl for medium.

## Ingredients

Exact measurements for everything, all from `pantry.md`. Grouped by where they go during prep.

Rice cooker:
- 2 rice-cooker cups basmati rice (using the plastic cup that came with the AROMA cooker; equals 1 1/2 regular measuring cups)
- Cold water filled to the cooker's line 2

Bowl 1, okra and onions (goes in first):
- 2 medium red onions (about 12 oz), peeled and cut into 1/2-inch pieces (about 2 cups)
- one 12 oz bag frozen cut okra, straight from the freezer
- 1 1/2 tbsp mustard oil
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp red chili powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp salt

Bowl 2, shrimp (goes in after 8 minutes):
- 1 1/2 lb frozen peeled, deveined shrimp, straight from the freezer
- 1 tbsp mustard oil
- 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1/2 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp red chili powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
- 1 tsp salt

To finish (by the air fryer and rice cooker, no bowl needed):
- 1 tbsp lemon juice (juice of 1/2 lemon)
- 1/4 cup chopped cilantro
- 1/2 tbsp unsalted butter (for the rice)
- Optional per meal: 2 tbsp yogurt on the side

## Prep (before any heat is on)

One line per bowl. Group everything that enters the appliance at the same moment into the same bowl (approaches.md rules 7 and 8).

- **Rice cooker first (before chopping):** Measure 2 rice-cooker cups of basmati rice (1 1/2 regular cups) into the rice cooker's inner pot. Cover with cold water, swirl for 10 seconds, pour off the water, and repeat 3 times total; drain well. Fill with cold water to the cooker's line 2, close the lid, and press White Rice (takes about 30 minutes and switches to Warm automatically).
- **Bowl 1 (okra and onions, large bowl):** Peel 2 medium red onions, chop them into 1/2-inch pieces (about 2 cups), and put them in a large bowl. Dump in the 12 oz bag of frozen cut okra straight from the freezer. Drizzle 1 1/2 tbsp mustard oil over top, then measure the spice mix directly onto the pile: 1 tsp coriander powder, 1 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp red chili powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric, and 1 tsp salt. Toss for 30 seconds until the onions and frozen okra are evenly coated.
- **Bowl 2 (shrimp, large bowl):** Put 1 1/2 lb frozen peeled, deveined shrimp straight from the freezer into a second large bowl. Add 1 tbsp mustard oil and 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, then measure the spice mix directly on top: 1 tsp coriander powder, 1/2 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp red chili powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp ground black pepper, and 1 tsp salt. Toss with a spoon for 30 seconds until every frozen shrimp is coated. Kept in a separate bowl because the shrimp need 8 minutes less cook time than the okra and onions.
- **By the appliances:** Have 1/2 lemon (for 1 tbsp juice), 1/4 cup chopped cilantro, and 1/2 tbsp unsalted butter ready on the counter.

## Steps

Every step self-contained: restate quantity, setting, temperature, and time inside the step.

1. Tip all of Bowl 1 [2 chopped red onions, 12 oz frozen cut okra, 1 1/2 tbsp mustard oil, 1 tsp coriander powder, 1 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp red chili powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp salt] into the 8 qt air fryer basket and shake once to spread into an even layer. Air fry at 400°F for 8 minutes, pulling the basket out to shake well at the 4-minute mark so the okra browns evenly.
2. Pull the air fryer basket out and dump all of Bowl 2 [1 1/2 lb frozen shrimp coated in 1 tbsp mustard oil, 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, 1 tsp coriander powder, 1/2 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp red chili powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp ground black pepper, 1 tsp salt] directly on top of the hot okra and onions, scraping in all the spice paste from the bowl. Shake the basket vigorously (or toss with a spatula) for 15 seconds so the shrimp, okra, and onions are mixed in a single loose layer across the wide 8 qt basket.
3. Air fry at 400°F for 7 minutes, pulling the basket out to shake midway at the 4-minute mark. The shrimp will be opaque and lightly charred at the edges, and the okra tender and blistered.
4. Off heat, squeeze 1 tbsp lemon juice (juice of 1/2 lemon) and scatter 1/4 cup chopped cilantro directly into the air fryer basket. Toss gently for 15 seconds.
5. Once the rice cooker has been on Warm for at least 5 minutes, open the lid, add 1/2 tbsp unsalted butter, and fluff with the paddle for 30 seconds. Portion into 3 containers: about 1 1/2 cups cooked rice and 1 1/2 cups spiced shrimp, okra, and onions per meal. Serve with 2 tbsp yogurt on the side if desired.

## Storage and reheating

- Cool the containers uncovered for 30 minutes, then lid and refrigerate for up to 3 days. Do not freeze (previously frozen shrimp turns rubbery after a second freeze).
- **Reheat in the microwave:** Sprinkle 1 tbsp water over the rice, rest the lid loosely on top, and microwave at 50% power for 2 minutes, then full power for 1 minute (using 50% power first keeps the shrimp from overcooking).
- **Optional air-fryer re-crisp for Days 2 and 3:** If you want the okra and shrimp crisp again, microwave just the rice with 1 tbsp water at full power for 90 seconds, and tip the cold shrimp and okra into the air fryer at 375°F for 3 minutes (no shake needed).
