# Bangladeshi-Style Beef Tehari with Potatoes

Beef cooked in the Instant Pot, rice in the rice cooker with some of the beef gravy, potatoes in the air fryer, and one fold at the end in the Instant Pot. Nothing gets fished out of a pot and nothing gets poured out and back.

- **Date:** 2026-10-18
- **Approach:** Weekend project
- **Appliances:** Instant Pot Duo 8 qt (beef, then the final mix), AROMA rice cooker (rice), Bella air fryer (potatoes)
- **Yield:** 8 meals, about 2 1/2 cups each (roughly 4 1/2 oz cooked beef and 3 oz potato per meal)
- **Hands-on time:** about 1 hour total: 10 minutes the night before for the marinade, about 55 minutes on cook day including portioning
- **Total time:** marinade 1 hour minimum (overnight is better), then about 2 1/4 hours on cook day; see the timeline

## Buy

- 3 lb beef stew meat, already cubed (at Walmart #2686, one "Beef Choice Angus Stew Meat Family Pack, 1.75 - 2.25 lb Tray" plus one "Beef Choice Angus Lean Stew Meat, Tray, 1.0 - 1.25 lb" adds up to about 3 lb)
- three 4 oz bags of serrano peppers
- one 1.5 lb bag of petite yellow potatoes

Everything else is in `pantry.md`: 4 red onions from the bag, 1 cup yogurt, 1/2 cup plus 1 tbsp mustard oil, 3 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, 3 regular cups basmati rice, ground and whole spices, salt, lemons, cilantro.

Notes before cooking:
- Why three appliances: rice sitting under thick gravy in the 8 qt Instant Pot trips its Burn sensor before pressure builds, because the wide base and long heat-up let the rice drink the liquid first. So the Instant Pot does the beef, the rice cooker does the rice, and the air fryer does the potatoes.
- The rice cooker takes at most 4 of its own cups of uncooked rice, which is 3 regular cups. That is why the rice is 3 cups; the potatoes make up the bulk.
- The rice cooks in 2 cups of the beef gravy plus water, so it tastes like tehari rice. Shortcut if you want 40 minutes back: start the rice cooker with plain water to line 4 the moment the Instant Pot lid goes on; the rice is plainer but everything still comes together at the end.
- Serranos go in with the beef, so the gravy carries the heat. Extra spicy as written. For plain hot, use 24 and slit 8.
- Red onions are the usual onion for this dish. Yellow work the same.
- Potatoes keep their skin. Rinse, halve, done.
- No tomatoes: tehari gets its tang from yogurt. Tehari is classically made with kalijira rice; basmati stands in.
- "Sauté on More" means: press Sauté, then press Sauté again (or Adjust on older Duos) until the "More" light is on.
- 6 bowls, each listed in Prep. None of them is touched after it goes in.

## Timeline (cook day)

## Ingredients

Grouped by the prep bowl they go into (see Prep below).

Bowl 1, marinade:
- 3 lb beef stew meat
- 1 cup yogurt
- 3 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 2 tsp red chili powder
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 2 tsp coriander powder
- 1 1/2 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp ground black pepper
- 1 tbsp salt

Bowl 2, whole spices:
- 3 bay leaves
- 6 cardamom pods
- 2 cinnamon sticks (about 2 inches each)
- 6 whole cloves

Bowl 3, onions:
- 4 medium red onions (about 1 1/2 lb), sliced 1/4 inch thick (about 6 cups)

Bowl 4, serranos:
- 36 serrano peppers (three 4 oz bags), stems on, 18 slit lengthwise, 18 left whole

Bowl 5, rice seasoning (small bowl):
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp garam masala

Bowl 6, potatoes:
- 1.5 lb petite yellow potatoes (the whole bag), rinsed, skin on, halved
- 1 tbsp mustard oil
- 1/2 tsp turmeric
- 1/2 tsp red chili powder
- 1/2 tsp salt

Rice cooker:
- 4 rice-cooker cups basmati (the cup that came with the cooker; this equals 3 regular cups), rinsed in the cooker pot
- 2 regular cups beef gravy, scooped from the Instant Pot after step 6
- water to the cooker's line 4

Straight into the Instant Pot:
- 1/2 cup mustard oil
- 1 cup water

To serve:
- 2 lemons, cut into quarters (1 quarter per meal)
- 1/2 cup chopped cilantro (1 tbsp per meal)

## Prep (before any heat is on)

- **Bowl 1 (marinade), large bowl, the night before or at least 1 hour ahead:** put in all 3 lb of beef. Add 1 cup yogurt, 3 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, 2 tsp red chili powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 2 tsp coriander powder, 1 1/2 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp ground black pepper, 1 tbsp salt. Stir with a spoon for 1 minute until every piece is coated. Cover and refrigerate at least 1 hour; overnight is better.
- **Bowl 2 (whole spices), small bowl:** 3 bay leaves, 6 cardamom pods, 2 cinnamon sticks, 6 cloves.
- **Bowl 3 (onions), large bowl:** peel 4 medium red onions, cut each in half from top to root, lay flat, slice 1/4 inch thick. About 6 cups.
- **Bowl 4 (serranos), medium bowl:** 36 serranos, stems on. Slit 18 of them lengthwise from the tip to just below the stem; leave 18 whole.
- **Bowl 5 (rice seasoning), small bowl:** 1 tsp salt, 1 tsp garam masala.
- **Bowl 6 (potatoes), large bowl:** rinse the whole 1.5 lb bag of potatoes under the tap, keep the skin on, cut each one in half. Add 1 tbsp mustard oil, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp red chili powder, 1/2 tsp salt. Toss with your hands for 20 seconds until the cut faces are coated.
- **By the Instant Pot:** 1/2 cup mustard oil in the measuring cup, 1 cup water in a glass.

## Steps

Every step self-contained: restate quantity, setting, temperature, and time inside the step.

1. Instant Pot on Sauté, set to More. Pour in 1/2 cup mustard oil. Heat for 3 minutes. It will shimmer and may smoke a little; that is expected.
2. Add Bowl 2 [3 bay leaves, 6 cardamom pods, 2 cinnamon sticks, 6 cloves]. Stir for 30 seconds.
3. Add Bowl 3 [4 sliced red onions, about 6 cups]. Sauté on More for 10 minutes, stirring every 2 minutes and scraping the bottom each time. They shrink and turn golden at the edges.
4. Add Bowl 1 [3 lb beef with all of its marinade]. Stir for 30 seconds to mix with the onions. Sauté on More for 6 minutes, stirring every 2 minutes.
5. Add 1 cup water and Bowl 4 [36 serranos, 18 slit, 18 whole]. Scrape the bottom for 30 seconds so nothing stays stuck. Press Cancel.
6. Lid on, valve to Sealing. Pressure Cook, High, 20 minutes. When it beeps, do nothing for 10 minutes (natural release), then turn the valve to Venting and wait until the pin drops (about 2 minutes). Open, then put the lid back on and press Keep Warm. The beef waits here, in its gravy, until the end.
7. Rice cooker. Measure 4 rice-cooker cups of basmati into the cooker's inner pot. Cover with cold water, swirl for 10 seconds, pour off, and repeat until rinsed 3 times; pour off as much water as you can. Open the Instant Pot, tilt it slightly, and dip the regular 1-cup measuring cup in at the edge twice to take 2 cups of gravy; a few beef pieces or onions coming along is fine. Pour both into the rice cooker pot. Add water up to the cooker's line 4 (4 because you used 4 cooker cups of rice). Add Bowl 5 [1 tsp salt, 1 tsp garam masala]. Stir once. Close the lid and press White Rice. About 35 to 40 minutes. Close the Instant Pot again, still on Keep Warm.
8. Potatoes, when the rice cooker has about 20 minutes left. Tip Bowl 6 [1.5 lb halved potatoes with 1 tbsp mustard oil, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 1/2 tsp red chili powder, 1/2 tsp salt] into the air fryer basket and spread them out. Air fry at 400°F for 15 minutes, shaking the basket at 8 minutes. A fork should slide into the biggest half with no resistance; if not, 3 more minutes at 400°F. Leave them in the basket.
9. When the rice cooker switches to Warm, leave it closed for 10 minutes. Then open it and fluff the rice with the spatula for 30 seconds.
10. Instant Pot lid off, still on Keep Warm. Add all the rice from the cooker and all the potatoes from the air fryer on top of the beef. Fold gently from the bottom with the spatula for 1 minute so beef, gravy, rice, and potatoes mix. Lid on, Keep Warm, 10 minutes so the rice drinks the gravy. If liquid pools when you tilt the pot after that, Sauté on Normal with the lid off for 3 minutes, stirring once, then Cancel.
11. Portion into 8 containers, about 2 1/2 cups each (2 level cups plus a heaped serving spoon), making sure each gets 5 or 6 potato halves. Eat one now with a quarter lemon squeezed over and 1 tbsp cilantro.

## Storage and reheating

- Cool uncovered for 30 minutes, then lid the containers. 6 meals go in the fridge (eat within 3 days). 2 meals go in the freezer the same day; keep up to 1 month. Frozen potatoes come back slightly mealy; in tehari that is fine.
- Reheat from the fridge: sprinkle 1 tbsp water over the meal, lid resting loosely on top, microwave full power 3 minutes, stir, then 1 more minute.
- Reheat from frozen: thaw in the fridge overnight, then reheat as above. In a hurry: microwave 50% power 6 minutes, stir, then full power 2 minutes.
- If a meal tastes flat, stir in 1/4 tsp salt and a squeeze of lemon after reheating.
