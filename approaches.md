# Approaches

How recipes must be written, and the named cooking modes to choose from. When asking for a recipe, name one approach. If none is named, assume **Hands-off batch**.

## Universal rules (apply to every approach)

1. **Measure everything.** Salt, spices, oil, water, rice, protein, vegetables. No "to taste", "a pinch", "a splash", or "until it looks right". Use tsp, tbsp, cups, and package units (lb, oz, cans). See `kitchen.md` for available tools.
2. **Every step is self-contained.** Each step restates the exact quantity, temperature, pressure setting, and time it needs. The cook should never have to look back at the ingredient list to execute a step.
3. **Appliance settings are explicit.** For the Instant Pot: mode (Sauté / Pressure Cook), pressure level, minutes, and release method with its duration. For the air fryer: °F and minutes. For the rice cooker: water-to-rice ratio and which cup measure.
4. **Only use what is in `pantry.md`.** If a dish really needs something missing, say so before the recipe and offer the closest in-stock substitute.
5. **State the yield.** Say how many meals the recipe makes and the approximate portion size per meal.
6. **Keep the ingredient list anyway.** It is useful for prep and shopping, but it is not a substitute for rule 2.

## Named approaches

### Hands-off batch

- **Goal:** cook once, eat for 3 days (6 meals: lunch and dinner).
- **Pot:** one pot only, the 8 qt Instant Pot. No second pan, no separate stovetop step.
- **Effort:** minimal babysitting. Load, set, walk away. A short Sauté step at the start is acceptable; constant stirring is not.
- **Time:** prefer under 15 minutes of hands-on time. Total time is less important than hands-on time.
- **Output:** a recipe that stores and reheats well across 3 days. Mention how to store and reheat.
- **Scaling:** size everything for 6 portions. Respect the Instant Pot max-fill line for the 8 qt model.
