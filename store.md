# Store

Where groceries come from. Rarely changes. Shopping is the exception: by default, cook from what is in `pantry.md`.

- **Store:** Walmart Neighborhood Market #2686, 1703 E Central Ave, Bentonville, AR 72712. The only store, within walking distance.

When a recipe needs something not in `pantry.md`, or the cook asks what to buy:

- Assume a standard American supermarket. Do not assume an Indian or international aisle. Anything that usually comes only from a specialty grocer (curry leaves, asafoetida, tamarind, dried fenugreek leaves) is optional at best: give the mainstream substitute and build the recipe on it.
- Never send the cook to another store, and never look up availability online. If unsure whether the store sells something, say "check at the store" in one phrase and move on.
- Shopping lists are short: only what the recipe needs, in package units (lb, oz, cans, bags), skipping what `pantry.md` already has.
