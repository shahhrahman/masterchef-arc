Where groceries come from. Rarely changes.

## Local store

The one grocery store within walking distance. Every shopping suggestion assumes this store and only this store.

- **Store:** Walmart Neighborhood Market #2686 (Bentonville E Central Ave Neighborhood Market)
- **Address:** 1703 E Central Ave, Bentonville, AR 72712
- **Hours:** closes 11 pm (seen 2026-10-17; check the store page for the current schedule)
- **Phone:** 479-418-0227
- **Store page:** https://www.walmart.com/store/2686-bentonville-ar

A Neighborhood Market is Walmart's small grocery format, not a Supercenter. Expect a standard American supermarket: produce, meat and seafood, dairy and eggs, frozen, canned and dry goods, and a mainstream spice rack. Do not assume an international or Indian aisle.

## Rules for anything not already in `pantry.md`

Apply these whenever a recipe needs something missing, or the cook asks what to buy.

1. **Assume a standard American supermarket.** Anything that usually comes only from an Indian, Asian, or other specialty grocer (for example curry leaves, asafoetida, tamarind, dried fenugreek leaves) is optional at best: mention it in one line, give the mainstream substitute, and build the recipe on the substitute.
2. **One store, no detours.** Never send the cook to another store (an Indian grocer, an Asian market, a Supercenter, online) unless they ask.
3. **Use the lists below.** Items under "Confirmed available" are safe to call for. Items under "Not carried" are off limits; use the substitute noted next to them.
4. **Shopping lists use package units** the store sells (lb, oz, cans, bags, count) and are grouped by store section: produce, meat and seafood, dairy and eggs, frozen, canned and dry goods, spices.
